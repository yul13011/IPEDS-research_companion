# What information is available in this IPEDS app?

This guide explains the local codebooks in everyday language. It is a starting point for choosing a question, not a promise that every calculation or institution/year combination is available.

Loaded source releases: 2015-16 Final, 2016-17 Final, 2017-18 Final, 2018-19 Final, 2019-20 Final, 2020-21 Final, 2021-22 Final, 2022-23 Final, 2023-24 Final, 2024-25 Provisional.

## Before you compare

- This is a guide to institution-level reports, not individual student records.
- A documented topic is not a guarantee that every field, year or institution has reported values.
- Release labels, fall snapshots, academic/fiscal periods and entering cohorts are different time concepts.
- Exact availability and calculation support must be checked against source fields and the coverage service.

## Institutions, programs and student services

School locations and characteristics, educational offerings, affiliations, special learning opportunities and reported student services.

Questions to try:
- Which Massachusetts colleges offer distance education?
- Does Smith College report on-campus childcare?

## Fall enrollment and student backgrounds

Fall student counts by level, attendance status and student characteristics, including race/ethnicity, gender, distance education, residence and some age information. Related tables cover retention and student-to-faculty ratios.

Questions to try:
- Show Yale's graduate enrollment by race and ethnicity.
- Convert those student counts into percentages of the matching population.

Example source documentation:
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · DRVEF2024 · Frequently used derived variables (EF): Fall enrollment 2024 (catalog record 135840)
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · EF2024 · Gender, attendance status, and level of student: Fall 2024 (catalog record 135833)
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · EF2024A · Race/ethnicity, gender, attendance status, and level of student: Fall 2024 (catalog record 135834)

## Enrollment across a full year

Twelve-month unduplicated student headcounts, distance education status, dual enrollment and instructional activity. Annual headcounts answer a different question from a fall snapshot.

Questions to try:
- How does annual enrollment differ from fall enrollment?
- What annual enrollment information is available?

Example source documentation:
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · DRVEF122024 · Frequently used derived variables (E12): 12-month enrollment, 2023-24 (catalog record 135832)

- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · EFFY2024_DIST · 12-month unduplicated headcount by distance education status and level of student: 2023-24 (catalog record 135830)

## Degrees, certificates and fields of study

Questions to try:

## Graduation rates and defined entering cohorts

Completion outcomes for specified entering cohorts and completion windows, including source-defined student groups and aid-recipient groups. The collection release and the year students entered are different dates.

Questions to try:
- Show Yale's six-year bachelor's graduation rate by entering cohort.
- Which entering-cohort years are available?

Example source documentation:
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · DRVGR2024 · Frequently used derived variables (GR) 150% of normal time to complete - cohort year 2017 (4-year) and cohort year 2020 (2-year) institutions (catalog record 135852)
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · GR200_24 · Graduation rate data, 200% of normal time to complete - cohort year 2016 (4-year) and cohort year 2020 (less-than-4-year) institutions (catalog record 135851)
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · GR2024 · Graduation rate data, 150% of normal time to complete - cohort year 2018 (4-year) and cohort year 2021 (2-year) institutions (catalog record 135848)

## Student financial assistance

Counts and amounts of grants, scholarships and loans from specified sources, including Pell grants and military/veterans benefits. First-time undergraduate and broader undergraduate populations must not be treated as interchangeable.

Questions to try:

## Institution finances

Institutional financial reporting uses different forms for public, private nonprofit and private for-profit institutions. Revenue, expenses and balance-sheet concepts must be interpreted within their source accounting definitions.

Questions to try:
- What financial reporting information is available for institutions?

Example source documentation:
- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · DRVF2024 · Frequently used/derived variables Finance (F): Fiscal year 2024 (catalog record 135858)

- IPEDS_2024-25_Provisional · F2324_F2 · Private not-for-profit institutions or Public institutions using FASB: Fiscal year 2024 (catalog record 135856)

# Tested faculty-answer examples

Evaluation only; live app unchanged.

## yale

**Question:** How many faculty does Yale have?

In our local IPEDS data, Yale University reported **5,735 staff with faculty status in Fall 2024**.

| Scope | Full-time | Part-time | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All staff with faculty status | 4,688 | 1,047 | 5,735 |
| Instructional staff with faculty status | 2,995 | 805 | 3,800 |

The first group counts everyone designated as faculty, whatever their job category, including research and public service roles. The second counts only faculty whose job is instructional, so it is a narrower, overlapping group.

Counts include medical-school staff where reported. Figures refer to Yale University as the reporting institution, not a separately identified college or school.

The rows overlap and must not be added together.

Source: local IPEDS Human Resources, Fall 2024, EAP2024; November 1 snapshot; provisional release.

## missing_school

**Question:** How many faculty are there?

Which institution do you mean? Please give its full name, and the campus if it is part of a larger system, so I can match it to a specific IPEDS reporting record.

## subdivision

**Question:** How many faculty does Yale College have?

Yale College may not have its own IPEDS staff record. Do you want faculty counts for Yale University as a whole, as reported in IPEDS, or did you mean a different institution?

## percentage

**Question:** What percentage of Yale faculty are tenured?

For Yale's Fall 2024 tenured share, should the denominator be all staff with faculty status (any occupation) or only instructional staff with faculty status? Should it include or exclude medical-school staff?

## ladder_salary

**Question:** How many ladder faculty does Yale have, and what is their average salary?

IPEDS salary data is by academic rank for full-time nonmedical instructional staff, and it can't be split by tenure status. Should I give the Yale headcount of tenured plus tenure-track instructional faculty and show the rank-based salary data as a separate, clearly labelled broader group, or do you want something else?

## future

**Question:** How many faculty will Yale have in Fall 2030?

IPEDS has no Fall 2030 data. Its latest available year is Fall 2024. Would you like Yale University's faculty headcount for Fall 2024 or another year from 2015 to 2024, instead of a 2030 projection?

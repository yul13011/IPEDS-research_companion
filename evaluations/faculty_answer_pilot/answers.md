# Proposed faculty-answer workflow: live pilot outputs

## yale

**Question:** How many faculty does Yale have?

In our local IPEDS data, Yale University reported **5,735 all staff with faculty status in Fall 2024**.

| Scope | Full-time | Part-time | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All staff with faculty status | 4,688 | 1,047 | 5,735 |
| Instructional staff with faculty status | 2,995 | 805 | 3,800 |

The lead scope counts every occupational category of staff who hold faculty status, so it can include people outside instruction, such as research or public service roles. The second scope narrows to instructional staff who hold faculty status. It is an overlapping subset of the first, so the two should not be added together. Both use the same combined full-time and part-time basis with the medical school included.

Counts include medical-school staff where reported. Figures refer to Yale University as the reporting institution, not a separately identified college or school.

The rows overlap and must not be added together.

Source: local IPEDS Human Resources, Fall 2024, EAP2024; November 1 snapshot; provisional release.

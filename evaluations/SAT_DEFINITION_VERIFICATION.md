# SAT population and reporting definition verification

The IPEDS ADM SAT measures concern enrolled first-time, degree/certificate-seeking undergraduate students who submitted relevant scores, including students entering in the summer before fall. For Yale, this is the same broad entering-undergraduate population described by “Yale College First Year Student SAT Scores.” These are not scores for all applicants, all admitted applicants, graduate students, or all undergraduate years.

The local ADM2017, ADM2018 and ADM2024 variable descriptions confirm first-time undergraduate scope. Historical 2017/2018 long descriptions contain older “SAT I Verbal” wording even where the field title has been updated; the survey instructions provide the fuller reporting protocol.

The official 2018–19 instrument, page 9 (PDF page index 8), specifies enrolled first-time degree/certificate-seeking students, inclusion of prior-summer entrants, and scores used in the admission decision. It also gives rules for students submitting SAT and ACT. Therefore a first-year SAT table's title alone does not establish identical score-selection rules or denominator coverage.

The official 2017–18 reporting-change notice and 2018–19 instrument require the new 2016 SAT scale and conversion of old SAT scores using College Board concordance tables. This confirms a substantive reporting-protocol change, beyond a renamed reading field. It does not prove that this caused each Yale/IPEDS discrepancy. The supplied Yale CSV has no footnote documenting its score-scale or selection treatment for those years.

Conclusion: broad student population is aligned; exact score treatment and reference-table comparability remain unverified. Preserve the five observed 2017/2018 score differences as numeric mismatches with a comparability warning. Do not switch to another student population, convert benchmark percentiles, or mark differences as matches without evidence.
